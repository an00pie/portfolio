Mode: Experience. Routes: /, /work, /work/:slug, /personal-gallery, /about, /contact.
Reference: user-confirmed video in design/, showing this-is-jony.framer.website.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The projects are objects in a floating, explorable room. The entry is the gallery itself, not a headline above a project grid.

OWN-WORLD: Reference-matched almost-white paper (#f7f7f5), near-black (#0d0e0c), thin light-gray rules, Spectral light serif display, Fragment Mono compact controls, a handwritten Anoop signature. Square image edges and spare typography; no neon effects or dark-theme utility overrides.

STORY: Enter the floating collection, select a project or choose work/gallery/about/contact, then read the existing real content in editorial pages. The black icon navigation expands into contextual links and local time/contact details.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-height white perspective room with 6–8 floating project/identity planes at different depths and orientations. A five-part navigation crosses the middle: gallery, work, Anoop, about, contact. Identity sits quietly at the lower edge. Image planes respond to pointer and drag; reduced motion gives a stable composition.

FORM: User-pinned Jony reference, no concept roll required. seed: none (user-pinned reference). Signature interaction: inertial perspective image field and black curtain page transitions. Gallery includes masonry/grid toggles and keyboard-operable image inspection. Subpages share the centered expandable icon dock.
